Delete your SeeNMeet account and data

Option 1: Delete it in the app

  1. Open SeeNMeet (Android app or app.getseenmeet.com) and log in.
  2. Go to Profile → Account Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

Option 2: Ask us to delete it

Email issac@donedealstudios.com from the email address on your account, with the subject "Delete my SeeNMeet account". We'll delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What gets deleted

Your account, profile details, photos, and intro video are permanently deleted. If you signed in with Facebook or Google, this also removes the data SeeNMeet received from that sign-in.

What we keep

Messages you sent stay visible to the people you sent them to, and reports involving your account may be kept for safety and to prevent abuse. Purchase records may be kept as required by law.
